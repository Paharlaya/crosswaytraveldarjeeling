About CrossWay Darjeeling Travel

Your Trusted Partner for Unforgettable Himalayan Journeys

Welcome to CrossWay Darjeeling Travel, your trusted partner for unforgettable Himalayan journeys. Based in the picturesque hill station of Darjeeling, West Bengal, we specialize in crafting exceptional travel experiences across the Eastern Himalayas. With years of industry expertise and a deep-rooted passion for hospitality, we are dedicated to showcasing the breathtaking beauty, rich culture, and warm traditions of this magnificent region to travelers from around the world.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for travel and a deep love for the Himalayas, CrossWay Darjeeling Travel has been creating unforgettable journeys since 2010. Our team of local experts knows every hidden gem, every scenic viewpoint, and every cultural experience that makes this region truly special.

Our Mission

To provide authentic, safe, and memorable travel experiences that connect people with the natural wonders and cultural heritage of the Himalayas.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and preferred travel partner in the region, known for quality service and genuine hospitality.

Core Values

Integrity • Customer First • Quality Service • Innovation • Sustainability
